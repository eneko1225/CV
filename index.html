About me

I was five years old when I sat down at my first computer and started typing MS-DOS commands. Robotics classes after school kept that curiosity alive, and when I wrote my first lines of code in high school I knew this was what I wanted to do. That led me to a higher degree in cross-platform application development, and I have been building software ever since.

Over the years I have worked across the whole stack: websites for Metro Bilbao and the Bilbao Fine Arts Museum, APIs for agencies and their clients, and three years of data and cloud services on AWS at BBVA Technology. Along the way I completed a specialization in Big Data and Artificial Intelligence.

Today I am Project Manager at INDALTER, where I lead a SaaS audio platform for retail from product definition all the way to deployment. I enjoy bridging business and engineering: understanding what a client needs, turning it into a clear plan, and making sure the team ships it well.
